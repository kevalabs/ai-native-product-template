# Backlog — the idea inbox

Dump raw ideas here (say `/idea <one line>` in Claude Code, or edit
this file directly). One line each, newest on top. No gate, no
format police — capturing an idea should take ten seconds.

Rules:

- An idea here is NOT work. It has no number, no promise, no owner.
- When an idea is worth doing, `/intent` graduates it into a numbered
  chain in `features/` and deletes its line here (git history keeps
  the record).
- Anyone can add. Deleting without graduating means we decided not to
  do it — say why in the commit message.
